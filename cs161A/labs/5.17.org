A tokenizer is a program that breaks a piece of text into its individual
pieces (called tokens). You have already used find() and substr() in the
find-and-replace lab; this lab uses the same two tools in a loop.

Write a program that takes one full line of text as input (use getline)
and prints each word of the sentence on its own numbered line, followed
by a count of the words. Words are separated by single spaces.

Requirements:
- Use find() to locate each space, and substr() to pull out the word
  before it.
- Keep going until there are no spaces left (remember: find() returns
  string::npos when it can't find anything). The remaining text is the
  last word.
- Do not use arrays or vectors; you only need a couple of string
  variables and a loop.

For example:

Enter a sentence: the cat sat on the mat
1: the
2: cat
3: sat
4: on
5: the
6: mat
6 words total

Hint: after you pull a word out of the front of the sentence, you can
replace the sentence with the part that comes after the space --
sentence = sentence.substr(spot + 1); -- so the loop always works on
what's left.
