Why These Topics Are Here

The following topics are part of this extended CS161A pass and will also be covered in much greater depth during CS161B:

  • Vectors: Dynamic arrays that grow and shrink as needed
  • Structs: Organizing related data into custom types
  • File I/O: Reading from and writing to files
  • Multi-file Programs: Breaking your code into organized modules
  • Algorithms: Sorting and searching techniques

Do not put pressure on yourself to become a wizard at all of this on the first pass. CS161B will come back to these ideas with more depth, more practice, and more demanding assignments.

These are real, useful tools, and if you're feeling steady with the earlier material, they are worth learning now.

This section is a bridge. A little exposure now makes the second pass later feel familiar.

And because the earlier chapters worked better when they started from complete programs, the next few sections will do the same thing: first a worked example, then the patterns and smaller snippets that fall out of it.
